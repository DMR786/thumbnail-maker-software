## 📊 Choosing the Right Preset

Three presets ship with the app. They trade speed against output quality.

| **Preset** | **Speed** | **Quality** | **VRAM** | **Best for** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast** | Fastest | Good | ~2 GB | Quick previews, large batches |
| **Balanced** | Medium | Very good | ~4 GB | Everyday work — start here |
| **Quality** | Slowest | Best | ~6 GB+ | Final output, print, archive |

**How to pick**

- Working on a laptop with integrated graphics? **Fast**.
- Mid-range GPU (GTX 1060 / RTX 3050)? **Balanced**.
- Desktop card with 8 GB+ VRAM? **Quality** for finals, **Balanced** while iterating.

**A practical workflow**

1. Run one file through **Fast** to check framing and settings.
2. Once it looks right, re-run the same file on **Quality**.
3. Batch the rest on **Quality** and leave it running.

That avoids spending an hour on a batch that had the wrong settings.

The app runs on almost anything, but the experience varies a lot.

| **Hardware** | **Typical speed** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **RTX 4060 / 8 GB** | Fastest | Quality preset comfortable |
| **GTX 1660 / 6 GB** | Fast | Balanced preset sweet spot |
| **Integrated (Iris Xe)** | Slow | Use Fast preset, batch overnight |
| **CPU only** | Slowest | Works, but plan for long runs |

**GPU vendors**

- **NVIDIA** — CUDA path, best performance.
- **AMD** — DirectML path, good performance.
- **Intel Arc / integrated** — DirectML, usable on newer chips.

**Practical tips**

- Keep the graphics driver current; older drivers lose 20–30% speed.
- Laptops on battery throttle the GPU — plug in for long jobs.
- Close other GPU-heavy applications (games, video editors) before a batch.
- Watch VRAM in Task Manager if you see out-of-memory errors.
